# 🏴‍☠️ Captain Cachebeard's Lost Treasure

## The Legend

For decades, sailors whispered about Captain Cachebeard, a pirate whose treasure was said to be hidden on an isolated island somewhere beyond the known trade routes.

Many searched for it.

None returned with proof.

What history forgot to mention was that, after retiring from piracy, Captain Cachebeard became fascinated by technology. He abandoned the sea and began building what he believed would become the most secure island in the world.

He studied servers instead of stars.

He trusted firewalls more than cannons.

He replaced treasure maps with deployment pipelines.

Unfortunately, he was a far better pirate than DevOps engineer.

---

## The Island

Hackademy Island was designed to protect the Captain's greatest treasure.

Instead, years of rushed deployments, forgotten backups, abandoned debugging tools, exposed repositories, and poor security decisions slowly turned the island into a puzzle waiting to be solved.

Every mistake tells part of the Captain's story.

Every vulnerability reveals another piece of the treasure map.

---

## Your Mission

You are not here to destroy the island.

You are an explorer following the trail left behind by Captain Cachebeard.

Search carefully.

Read everything.

Question every assumption.

The treasure is never hidden behind impossible tricks—it is hidden behind realistic mistakes.

---

# The Journey

## 🏝 Forgotten Harbor

The Captain never deleted old backup archives.

Your adventure begins by exploring the abandoned harbor, where forgotten files still drift ashore.

**Lesson:** Exposed backup files and directory enumeration.

---

## 🏠 Captain's Cabin

Inside the Captain's private cabin lies a maintenance console that should never have reached production.

Someone forgot to clean up after deployment.

**Lesson:** Information disclosure through exposed internal tools.

---

## 👻 Ghost Ship

The Captain believed deleting a file erased it forever.

Git remembers differently.

The abandoned Ghost Ship hides secrets buried deep within its history.

**Lesson:** Version control history and repository exposure.

---

## 🗼 Watch Tower

The guards trusted anyone claiming to be friendly.

The Watch Tower trusted anyone claiming to be local.

Sometimes, identity is just another HTTP header.

**Lesson:** Reverse proxy trust and forwarded headers.

---

## 💰 Treasure Vault

The Captain stored his greatest secrets in what he believed was a secure vault.

Secrets are only as safe as the application protecting them.

**Lesson:** Secret management and configuration security.

---

# Captain Cachebeard's Creed

> "A careless pirate loses gold.
>
> A careless engineer loses everything."

---

# Educational Goals

Every challenge in this CTF is inspired by a real class of security mistake.

Participants are encouraged to:

* Think methodically.
* Enumerate before exploiting.
* Read source code and documentation.
* Understand why the vulnerability exists.
* Learn how to prevent it in real systems.

The objective is not simply to capture flags, but to leave with practical knowledge that applies outside the CTF.
